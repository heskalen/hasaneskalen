About Me

My research focuses on materials science and engineering, particularly the synthesis and characterization of nanomaterials, functional glass systems, biopolymer-based composites, and applied materials research.

I conduct teaching and research activities in the Opticianry Program, Department of Medical Services and Techniques, Onikişubat Vocational School at Kahramanmaraş Sütçü İmam University, Türkiye.
