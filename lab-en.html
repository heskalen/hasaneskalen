RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

What we explore

Our work connects experimental materials research, characterization, and application-oriented scientific questions.

01 / 06

Functional Glasses & Radiation Shielding

Composition–structure–property relationships in borate-based glasses, with optical, physical, mechanical, and radiation-shielding performance.

02 / 06

Carbon Quantum Dots (CQDs)

Synthesis, fluorescence properties, and potential sensing applications of carbon-based functional nanomaterials.

03 / 06

Biopolymers & 2D Nanocomposites

Functional chitosan- and starch-based films incorporating layered double hydroxides, g-C₃N₄, and other nanofillers.

04 / 06

Corrosion & Surface Protection

Exploratory work on nanomaterial-based corrosion inhibitors and partnerships for electrochemical characterization.

05 / 06

Photocatalysis & Environmental Applications

Light-driven photocatalytic behavior of nanocomposites, dye degradation, and related functional material applications.

06 / 06

Optical Characterization & Data-Driven Analysis

UV–Vis, photoluminescence, structural and morphological analysis, and collaborative modeling of material properties.
